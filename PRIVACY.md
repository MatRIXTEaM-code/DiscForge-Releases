# DiscForge and your privacy

DiscForge doesn't collect, store or send any personal information. There are no adverts, no
tracking and no account.

## What stays on your PC

Your discs, images, settings and DiscForge's log stay on your computer. Settings and the log are in
`%AppData%\DiscForge`. You can delete that folder at any time.

## What goes over the internet

- **Update check.** Once a day (unless you turn it off in Settings), DiscForge asks GitHub for the
  newest version number. Like any web request, this shows GitHub your IP address. Nothing else is
  sent. Updates are downloaded from GitHub too.
- **Problem reports.** Only sent if you send them yourself. "Report a problem" fills in a report on
  GitHub in your browser and shows you exactly what's in it first; you can change or remove
  anything, or not send it. Your Windows user name, computer name and email addresses are left out
  of reports and diagnostics files. GitHub reports are public, and GitHub's privacy policy applies
  to them. To have a report removed, email us (below), or delete it yourself on GitHub. You can also
  report a problem by email instead of GitHub.
- **Donations** go through PayPal, and PayPal's privacy policy applies.
